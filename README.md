# Superstore Sales Analysis
 
## Dataset
Kaggle Superstore Dataset
 
## Business Questions
 
### Which regions generate the most revenue?
West region generated the highest revenue followed by East.
 
### Which regions generate the most profit?
West region generated the highest profit.
 
### Which products are most profitable?
Copiers, Phones and Accessories generated the strongest profits.
 
### Which products lose money?
Tables and Bookcases generated negative profitability.
 
### How do discounts affect profitability?
Profitability decreases significantly as discount levels increase.
 
### Which customers create the most value?
The top 10 customers generated a disproportionate share of total profit.
 
### What trends exist over time?
Sales display an overall upward trend with seasonal spikes.
 
### What recommendations would improve profit?
 
- Reduce excessive discounting.
- Review unprofitable product categories.
- Focus sales efforts on high-margin products.
- Retain top-profit customers.
